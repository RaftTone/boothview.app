Right of withdrawal (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the law normally gives you 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content, that right ends once delivery has begun, if you agreed to it. At checkout we therefore ask you to confirm that you want your license key delivered immediately and that you understand you lose your 14-day right of withdrawal once it is delivered. We include a confirmation of this consent in your order email.

If this consent was not obtained for your order, your 14-day right of withdrawal applies as usual: write to us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you.
