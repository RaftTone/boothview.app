Refund Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026.

14-day money-back guarantee

If Boothview doesn't work for you, write to us within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you in full. You don't need to give a reason, though we'd like to know what went wrong.

How to ask for a refund

Email boothviewapp@gmail.com with:

  • the email address you used for the purchase;
  • the order number from your confirmation email, or the date and amount of the payment.

We reply within 2 business days.

How the refund is paid

  • The refund goes back to the card you paid with, through WayForPay. We can't refund to a different card or account.
  • You were charged in Ukrainian hryvnias (UAH), and we refund the exact UAH amount charged. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts it again, so the amount you get back may differ slightly from what you paid because of exchange rates. We don't control this.
  • Depending on your bank, the money usually reaches your card within 3 to 30 days after we issue the refund.

After a refund

Your license key and download link stop working, and you must stop using Boothview and delete it.

After 14 days

We don't normally refund purchases more than 14 days old. If Boothview stops working because of a fault on our side that we can't fix, write to us anyway and we'll find a fair solution.

Cancelling an order

A payment that has not gone through yet (for example, still pending at your bank) can be cancelled. Write to us and we'll cancel it.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank. We'll refund you faster than a chargeback would.
