A license key is digital content, delivered as soon as your payment goes through. Once your key has been issued, we don't refund the purchase, except in the cases below. That is why the trial is a full one.

If Boothview has a fault, or doesn't do what boothview.app says it does, write to us. We'll try to fix it first. If we can't fix it within a reasonable time, we'll refund you in full. ## Right of withdrawal (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the law normally gives you 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content, that right ends once delivery has begun, if you agreed to it. At checkout we therefore ask you to confirm that you want your license key delivered immediately and that you understand you lose your 14-day right of withdrawal once it is delivered. We include a confirmation of this consent in your order email.

If this consent was not obtained for your order, your 14-day right of withdrawal applies as usual: write to us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you.

- The refund goes back to the card you paid with, through WayForPay. We can't refund to a different card or account.

- Depending on your bank, the money usually reaches your card within 3 to 30 days after we issue the refund.
